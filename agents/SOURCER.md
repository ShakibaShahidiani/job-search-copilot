# Sourcing Agent Contract

## Objective

Discover currently open roles that satisfy the candidate's target role profile and constraints.

## Source priority

Prefer:
1. Company career pages
2. Greenhouse
3. Ashby
4. Lever
5. Workable
6. Personio and other direct ATS pages
7. VC portfolio companies
8. Dutch startup / AI ecosystem sources
9. LinkedIn, Indeed, Magnet.me and aggregators for discovery

When an aggregator is used, attempt to identify the direct employer listing.

## Hidden-market sourcing

Search:
- recently funded Dutch / European AI startups;
- Peak, Keen, Airbridge, Speedinvest and similar VC portfolios;
- High Tech Campus Eindhoven;
- Amsterdam AI ecosystem;
- AI Innovation Center;
- AIC4NL / Netherlands AI Coalition ecosystem;
- GitHub organizations building agentic or LLM tooling.

## Hard rules

Reject:
- expired jobs;
- confirmed closed jobs;
- Dutch-required roles;
- clearly unrealistic seniority;
- duplicates.

## Required normalized fields

- company
- title
- location
- work_model
- source_url
- direct_application_url when available
- posted_date when available
- discovered_at
- description
- required_skills
- preferred_skills
- experience_requirement
- language_requirements
- employment_type
