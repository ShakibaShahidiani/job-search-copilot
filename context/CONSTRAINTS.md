# Search Constraints

## Geography

Priority order:
1. Utrecht
2. Amsterdam
3. Eindhoven / Brainport
4. Rotterdam
5. Other Netherlands locations
6. Netherlands remote
7. EU remote where Dutch residence is acceptable

Hybrid is acceptable.
Remote is acceptable.

## Language

Hard reject by default:
- Dutch required
- Dutch fluency mandatory
- Native Dutch required

Do not reject:
- Dutch preferred
- Dutch is a plus
- Dutch optional

## Seniority

Preferred:
- Junior
- Early-career
- Medior / Mid-level

Stretch applications are allowed when:
- the technical fit is unusually high; and
- the experience requirement is moderately above the candidate profile.

Strongly down-rank:
- 5+ years mandatory unless the role appears flexible
- Senior roles with clear leadership ownership
- Staff / Principal / Head roles

## Work authorization

Prefer roles that can hire a candidate already authorized to work in the Netherlands.

Do not assume employer sponsorship is needed.

## Job freshness

Prefer roles posted or refreshed within 30 days.

Roles older than 60 days should usually be rejected unless the company career page confirms they remain actively open.

## Quality rules

Do not include a job merely because the title matches.

A good recommendation must have:
- a plausible seniority fit;
- meaningful technical overlap;
- acceptable language requirements;
- valid location/work arrangement;
- evidence that the role is still open.
